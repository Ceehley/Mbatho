HOW WE CAN HELP YOU





Mbatho Group (Pty) Ltd is a proudly South African black owned company that specializes in multiple industries ranging from security services to making trailers and more. Our Trailers are very diverse and well made, our catalog of trailers includes diesel bowers, cargo trialers , light trailers, water pump trailers, fridge trailers, toilet trailers. Name it and we will build it. Mbatho Group also provides courier services and other services as specified in our website. We have been providing quality service to our customers since 2015 in Gauteng, Mpumalanga and Limpopo . Mbatho Group is Registered under PSIRA and the National Bargining Council For the Private Security Sector . Our headquarters is in Germiston Our security forces consists of a trained guarding force, a fleet of vehicles, and a 24/7 control room. we also offer guarding, consulting, technical services, risk assessments, crowd control, special events, escort services, VIP protection, tactical and armed response.
